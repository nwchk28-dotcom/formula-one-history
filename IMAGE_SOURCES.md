# Champion image sources

The 35 champion images in `public/champions/` were downloaded from Formula 1's official Hall of Fame page on 2026-08-26:

- https://www.formula1.com/en/drivers/hall-of-fame

They are used for this local design prototype. Formula 1 and Getty Images retain their respective rights. Confirm publication and licensing rights before a public or commercial release.

Each filename identifies the driver pictured. The site reuses that driver's official Hall of Fame image for every season they won the Drivers' Championship.
